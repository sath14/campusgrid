How It Works
1
Tap in
Student taps their existing campus ID on the doorway RFID reader — no new card needed.
2
Instant sync
Tag ID, timestamp, and door location sync to the CampusGrid backend in real time.
3
Camera cross-check
An overhead camera counts bodies crossing the doorway — no facial recognition, just a headcount — and compares it against the RFID tap count for that minute.
✓
Auto-logged, or flagged
Matching counts log attendance automatically. A mismatch (e.g. 2 taps, 1 body) flags both tags for review — catching shared or borrowed cards.
