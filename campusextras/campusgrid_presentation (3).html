01 — The Problem
Part 1 of 5 Speaker 1

Campus life runs on scattered systems.

"It's your first week. You've got a timetable PDF, three group chats, a printed map, and you're still late for your first class."

A

New students and visitors rely on word-of-mouth or guesswork to find lecture halls, labs, and offices across sprawling campus blocks.

B

Attendance is still largely manual — roll calls eat into class time and leave records prone to error.

C

Facility status, event schedules, and accessibility routes live in disconnected group chats, noticeboards, and PDFs.

D

03 — Six Pillars
Part 2 of 5 Speaker 2

One grid, six routes to campus.

PILLAR 01

Navigation

Turn-by-turn indoor & outdoor routing between any two points on campus.

PILLAR 02

Directory

A living index of rooms, staff, labs, and services — searchable in seconds.

PILLAR 03

AI Assistant

Ask in plain language — "where's my next class" — and get a route, not a link.

PILLAR 04

Accessibility

Routes that account for mobility needs, ramps, and lift access by default.

PILLAR 05

Real-Time Data

Live facility status, room availability, and event updates as they happen.

PILLAR 06

Sustainability

Route suggestions and usage data that support walkability and lower energy load.
