How this prints as one seamless poster

Each of the 3 posters below is split into 4 A4 sheets (a 2×2 grid) — 12 sheets total. Print at 100% scale, no "fit to page". Every sheet has a 12mm margin on all sides — cut each sheet along its four corner crop marks to remove that margin, then tape the trimmed edges together in this arrangement:

R1·C1R1·C2
R2·C1R2·C2

Each assembled poster comes out to roughly 37cm × 55cm. The small gray label at the bottom of each sheet is inside the margin and gets trimmed away — it's only there to help you keep the sheets in order.
